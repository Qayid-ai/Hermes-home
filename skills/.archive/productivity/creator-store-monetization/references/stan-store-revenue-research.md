# Stan Store Revenue Research

## Source Use

This reference captures a May 2026 research pass on Stan Store and adjacent digital product markets. Treat prices and product examples as market observations, not permanent facts. Re-check current Stan docs before configuring an account.

## Stan Capabilities

Stan Store supports:

- Digital downloads: PDFs, templates, spreadsheets, assets, MP4, SVG, PSD, EPS, XLS, etc.
- URL/media products: external links, hosted resources, Notion/Canva/Drive links.
- eCourses: modules, lessons, uploaded videos, lesson descriptions, supporting files, drip/draft/publish controls.
- Lead magnets / collect emails: free products with file or redirect URL delivery.
- Coaching calls: paid appointments with Google Calendar and Zoom options.
- Webinars: paid/free group sessions with Google Meet, Zoom, or custom location.
- Recurring memberships.
- Community: one community per Stan account in the researched docs.
- Custom products: manual fulfillment such as audits, reports, reviews, custom files.

## Stan Constraints Found

- Creator plan: $29/mo or $300/year.
- Creator Pro: $99/mo or $948/year.
- Stan platform transaction fee: 0%.
- Stripe/PayPal processing fees still apply.
- Digital download recommended size: <=500MB.
- Digital download maximum: 5GB.
- Course lesson video maximum: 4GB each.
- Lead magnet confirmation email needs a file or URL.
- Customer file uploads at checkout were not supported in docs checked.
- Creator Pro needed for discount codes, limited quantity offers, payment plans, order bumps, affiliates, email broadcasts/flows, imported contacts, and tracking pixels.
- Membership billing dates are based on purchase date; not manually set.
- Deleting a membership product or cancelling Stan does not automatically cancel customer subscriptions.

## Product Market Read

Observed saturated product classes across Stan/Gumroad/Payhip/Etsy-style markets:

- Generic ChatGPT prompt packs.
- Massive AI prompt bundles.
- Generic Canva social template packs.
- Generic digital planners and PLR planner bundles.
- Generic "make money with AI" guides.
- AI image prompt bundles without a specific use case.

Observed better differentiated classes:

- Vertical-specific AI kits: dentist, realtor, teacher, coach, med spa, service business.
- Outcome-bound reports and audits.
- Workflow packs that include prompts + checklist + templates + example output.
- Notion/Canva systems aimed at a specific job, not generic productivity.
- Live or manual implementation offers layered behind a digital product.
