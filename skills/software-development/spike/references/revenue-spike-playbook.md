# Revenue Spike Playbook

Use this reference when the user asks the agent to "start a business", "make revenue", or prove commercial ability quickly.

## Core principle

Do not claim revenue until money is collected or a payment/contract is confirmed. A revenue spike validates the shortest paid path, not a full company.

## Fast sequence

1. Pick a narrow buyer with public lead data and urgent measurable pain.
2. Ground the pain with at least two sources:
   - one authoritative or official source for macro/context
   - one niche/source-proximate signal for buyer pain
3. Choose a productized service before SaaS when speed matters.
   - Fixed scope
   - Fixed price
   - 48h-7d delivery window
   - Refund/guarantee only if fulfilment risk is controlled
4. Build sales assets before product depth:
   - one-page landing page
   - cold email
   - call opener
   - lead CSV
   - fulfilment runbook
5. Verify the asset locally:
   - HTTP 200 if served
   - key CTA and price are present
   - lead CSV has usable rows
6. Stop before outreach if identity, payment link, or permission is missing.

## Good first offers

- Audit: missed calls, booking leakage, review leakage, local SEO leakage, ad spend leakage.
- Implementation sprint: fix the top 3-5 issues found in the audit.
- Monitoring: small monthly retainer for alerts/reporting.

## Output shape

Create a small folder/package with:

```text
index.html
assets/
ops/leads.csv
ops/runbook.md
outreach/cold-email.txt
outreach/call-script.txt
```

## Pitfalls

- Do not build SaaS first. Distribution is the constraint.
- Do not call generic "AI automation" an offer. Tie it to a loss metric.
- Do not send cold outreach from the user's identity without explicit approval.
- Do not rely only on AI-content-farm market claims. Treat commercial stats as directional unless methodology is clear.
- Do not confuse a launched page with revenue.
